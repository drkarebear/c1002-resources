About This Site

Privacy Policy

Created by Karen Crozer for ENGL C1002 classes.

Short version: These Literary Analysis Labs do not collect, store, or submit student responses or personal information.

What This Site Does Not Collect

This site does not ask you to create an account or provide your name, email address, student ID, or other personal information.

The activities do not send your answers, choices, reflections, or completion information to Karen Crozer or to a course database.

The site does not use its own analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, or tracking scripts.

Activity Responses and Reflections

Your activity choices are used only in your browser so the page can show feedback and move you through the activity.

If an activity gives you a reflection prompt, the reflection page asks you to write your response in Canvas or wherever your instructor directs you. The Literary Analysis Labs do not collect that reflection.

If your instructor asks for a screenshot of a completion screen, you decide where to submit that screenshot according to your course instructions. The website itself does not receive it.

Website Hosting

This site is hosted using GitHub Pages. Like most web-hosting services, GitHub may process standard technical information needed to deliver web pages, such as network requests and security-related logs, under GitHub's own policies. That hosting information is separate from these Literary Analysis Labs and is not a record of your answers to the activities.

Questions

If you are using these pages in a class and have a question about an assignment or what you are expected to submit, follow the directions in your Canvas course or ask your instructor.
