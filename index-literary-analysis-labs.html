Literary Analysis Labs

Short, accessible interactive activities created by Karen Crozer to help students practice the thinking moves behind literary analysis.

About These Labs

Literary analysis can feel overwhelming when several skills are expected at once. These labs break the process into smaller moves so you can practice one skill at a time, get immediate feedback, and try again without penalty.

The collection may be especially helpful in ENGL C1002: Introduction to Literature, where students develop close-reading and analytical writing skills. The same skills can also support students in upper-level English courses that require literary analysis, including ENGL 218: Children’s Literature.

These are practice spaces, not tests. You do not need to complete every lab in one sitting. Work through the sequence or jump directly to the skill you want to practice.
1. NoticeSee what the text is doing.
2. InterpretAsk what the pattern might mean.
3. SupportUse evidence and explain it.
4. DevelopBuild and complicate an argument.
