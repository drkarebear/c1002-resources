A Useful Evidence Pattern

Context → focused quotation → unpack the language → connect it to the claim. Signal-phrase punctuation depends on the grammar of your sentence: a short phrase can be embedded with no comma, a speaking signal phrase often takes a comma before a full quoted sentence, and a colon can introduce a quotation after a complete sentence. If your class uses MLA, place the parenthetical citation where your instructor requires it and keep the analytical explanation in your own voice.
