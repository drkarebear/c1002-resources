Literary Analysis Lab

So What?

A useful analytical question opens the door. The next step is to consider a possible interpretation: an explanation of what the detail may suggest and why it matters.

What This Helps You Do

You will practice moving from something you notice, to something you wonder, to an interpretation that stays close to the evidence.

NOTICE What pattern, contrast, word, action, or detail stands out?
→
WONDER Why might that detail matter?
→
INTERPRET What could the detail suggest?
A good interpretation is not just “deep sounding.” It should grow from the evidence and remain possible to defend with the text.

A useful interpretation usually does three things:

  • grows from something specific in the text,
  • answers a meaningful question about that detail, and
  • stays close enough to the evidence that you could defend it.

You do not need to prove that your interpretation is the only possible reading. You need to show why it is a reasonable reading of the text.

This low-stakes practice activity does not record or submit student responses.
