Move 4 of 4

4. Develop

Build and complicate an argument. Use these activities to turn a first interpretation into a stronger argument by sharpening claims, revising for complexity, and repairing weak analysis.

Choose one. Pick the activity that would help you most right now, unless your instructor assigned a specific one.

Develop Activities

Develop activity 1

Strengthen the Thesis

Move from topic and observation toward a specific, arguable interpretive thesis.

If this sounds like you:
“My thesis sounds broad, obvious, or like a formula.”

Open “Strengthen the Thesis”
Develop activity 2

Complicate It

Revise an interpretation when new evidence adds tension or contradiction.

If this sounds like you:
“Some of the evidence doesn’t fit my claim.”

Open “Complicate It”
Develop activity 3

The Analysis Repair Shop

Diagnose the biggest analytical gap inside one literary-analysis paragraph and choose the highest-impact repair.

If this sounds like you:
“My paragraph isn’t working, but I don’t know what is wrong.”

Open “The Analysis Repair Shop”
Develop activity 4

Titles, Introductions & Conclusions

If this sounds like you:
“I have the body of my essay, but I don’t know how to start it, end it, or title it without sounding generic.”

Practice framing a literary-analysis essay without relying on broad hooks, plot-summary introductions, or conclusions that only repeat the thesis.

Open “Titles, Introductions & Conclusions”
Develop activity 5

Ask a Deeper Question

If this sounds like you:
“I know my text is about power, identity, belonging, or another big idea, but my argument still feels shallow.”

Practice turning a broad observation into a deeper question about how the idea works, who it affects, what tension it creates, and what the text may be suggesting.

Open “Ask a Deeper Question”
Develop activity 6

What Should This Paragraph Prove?

If this sounds like you:
“I have a thesis, but I do not know what each body paragraph is supposed to do.”

Practice turning a thesis into smaller paragraph claims, writing analytical topic sentences, and matching each paragraph job to evidence.

Open “What Should This Paragraph Prove?”
