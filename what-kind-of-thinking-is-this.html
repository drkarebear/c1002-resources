Literary Analysis Lab

What Kind of Thinking Is This?

Literary analysis gets easier when you can tell the difference between telling what happened, noticing something in the text, and exploring what that detail might mean.

What This Helps You Do

You will practice three different kinds of thinking that often appear in writing about literature. None of them is “bad.” The key is knowing which one you are using.

Tells What Happened Summary: reports events, actions, or information from the text.
Notices Something Observation: points out a pattern, choice, contrast, repetition, or other detail.
Explores What It Might Mean Analysis: explains what a detail or pattern may suggest and why it matters.
No score. This is practice. You can try every question again, and the feedback will explain the thinking behind each answer.
