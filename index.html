Literary Analysis Labs

Short, interactive practice activities created by Karen Crozer to help students build the thinking skills they need for literary analysis.

About These Labs

These resources are designed to help students practice the key moves of literary analysis one step at a time. Instead of asking you to write a whole essay at once, each lab focuses on one small skill, such as noticing a pattern, asking a useful question, or explaining why a detail matters.

The labs may be especially helpful in ENGL C1002: Introduction to Literature, where students develop close-reading and analytical writing skills. They can also support students in upper-level English courses that require literary analysis, including ENGL 218: Children’s Literature.

These are practice spaces, not tests. You can retry questions, read the feedback, and work at your own pace.
