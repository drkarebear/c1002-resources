Literary Analysis Lab

Pattern Finder

Literary analysis often begins when several small details start to point in the same direction. This lab helps you decide which details belong together and what kind of pattern they create.

What This Helps You Do

Instead of treating each quotation as a separate island, look for relationships among details.

1. CollectNotice several details from the text.
2. GroupDecide which details belong together.
3. Name the PatternAsk what the group may reveal about the text.
A pattern is more than repetition. Details can belong together because they repeat, contrast, develop, or create tension with one another.

DETAILS FROM THE TEXT

You practiced finding patterns across details.

When you review your notes or annotations, try asking:

  • Which details repeat?
  • Which details contrast with one another?
  • Which details seem to change together?
  • Which details point toward the same larger idea?

A strong interpretation often begins when separate details stop looking separate.
