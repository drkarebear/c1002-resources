4. Develop

Ask a Deeper Question

Noticing that a text is “about power” or “about identity” is a useful start. Development happens when you ask a question that makes the idea more specific, complicated, and arguable.

Move from topic to problem. Instead of asking only “Where is power?” ask how power works, who benefits, who resists it, what it costs, and what the text seems to suggest about that dynamic.

Broad observation: There is a power dynamic in this relationship.

Better question: Who has power, and how do we know?

Deeper question: How is that power maintained or challenged, and what does the text suggest about why the relationship continues?
